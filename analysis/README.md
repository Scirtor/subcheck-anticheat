# Survey and interview analysis

`survey_aggregates.csv` is the canonical transcription of 126 option counts across 23 unique closed questions. Percent = count / question base × 100. Bases are 20, 19 or 9. Counts from small pie slices are inferred from the plotted shares and base. Zero categories are inferred from the absence of a slice or a labelled zero. Multiple-selection totals may exceed the base and cannot be added as unique people.

`survey_aggregates.json` contains the same source counts. `open_feedback.csv` contains visible answer cards only, with reported and visible totals. Card numbers identify screenshot order, not respondent IDs. Q14=Q13 and Q19=Q03 are omitted as duplicate images. No participant-level correlations, cross-tabs or overlap estimates are possible.

Summary calculations: seriousness mean = (3×2 + 4×7 + 5×10) / 19 = 4.42; satisfaction mean = (1×1 + 2×5 + 3×8 + 4×4 + 5×1) / 19 = 2.95; usefulness mean = (3×4 + 4×8 + 5×8) / 20 = 4.20. Top-two seriousness = 17/19 = 89.5%; usefulness = 16/20 = 80%; frequent or occasional encounters = 14/20 = 70%.

Interview themes and decisions are discussed in the report and timestamped evidence notes. `templates/response_collection_template.xlsx` is the former empty collection workbook, preserved as an unused template. It is not research data.
