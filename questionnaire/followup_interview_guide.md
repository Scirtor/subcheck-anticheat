# Follow-up interview guide

Purpose: test customer jobs, pains and gains with actual game owners and moderators. This is a proposed guide, not a claim that the completed recordings followed every question.

1. What is your role, game genre, stage of development and responsibility for multiplayer security?
2. Tell us about the last suspected cheating incident. What happened?
3. What did you do, which tools did you use and how long did it take?
4. What evidence led to a decision? What happened if the decision was challenged?
5. What was the hardest part of the process? What outcome would have helped?
6. Describe your latest integration of a security tool. Who approved it and what effort did it require?
7. Which game events can your authoritative server export today? What would additional collection change?
8. What restrictions apply to data storage, transfer and external processing?
9. Who owns the budget and what are you currently spending or allocating to this task?
10. After discovery, present SUB-CHECK as a proposed supplementary risk-scoring tool. What concerns would prevent a pilot?
11. What measurable evidence would justify continuing after a pilot?
12. Can we agree on a follow-up with a data owner, permitted sample and review workflow?

Request recording permission. Ask about past behaviour before introducing the concept. Use neutral probes, avoid arguing and thank respondents. Target 15–30 minutes. Record role, source timestamp, reported behaviour, interpretation and uncertainty separately.
